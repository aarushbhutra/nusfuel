# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Platform Notes

NUSFuel ships on Android and iOS. The Expo web target is for development and testing only, not a product platform.

## Users

Gym-focused NUS students using Techno Edge who want to log campus meals and stay aware of calorie and protein targets.

## Product Purpose

NUSFuel helps students set daily nutrition goals, log stored Techno Edge meals with adjustable servings, track progress, and choose the next meal with grounded recommendations.

## Positioning

The focused Techno Edge meal companion for NUS students who want meal logging and practical calorie and protein guidance without invented nutrition facts.

## Brand Personality

Smooth, minimal, elegant.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic, boring, messy, or confusing interfaces.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Keep goal setting and meal logging quick to understand.
- Use calm hierarchy and consistent controls across screens.
- Treat stored nutrition and allergen data as the source of truth.
- Keep recommendations helpful without making medical or safety guarantees.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA contrast, readable type, reduced-motion support, and screen-reader labels. No additional requirements are currently specified.
